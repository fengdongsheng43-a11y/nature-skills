# nature-nsfc-proposal

Draft v0.1.0. National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) research-to-grant decision workflow: official scheme eligibility, literature and novelty audit, rival hypotheses, decisive experiments, applicant-controlled composition support, and reviewer/compliance checks.

Science is governed by [nature-research-partner](../nature-research-partner/SKILL.md), while argument architecture and revision are routed to [nature-proposal-writer](../nature-proposal-writer/SKILL.md). NSFC's **Regional Science Fund** belongs to this skill, not the provincial/municipal fund skill.

Start with [SKILL.md](SKILL.md), [annual rules](references/annual-guide-intake.md), [review gates](references/reviewer-gates.md), and [workflow rationale](../../docs/funding-workflow-architecture.md). Always verify the call-year NSFC guide and official scheme template. The applicant remains responsible for compliance and final writing.
