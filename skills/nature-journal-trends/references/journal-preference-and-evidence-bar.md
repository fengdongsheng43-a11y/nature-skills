# Journal publishing signals and changing evidence standards

## Three-source ledger

Keep **OFFICIAL** (live scope/guidelines/editorials), **OBSERVED** (published
articles), and **REJECTION** (rare verified rejection guidance; otherwise
weakly sourced anecdotes) separate. A published corpus is conditioned on
submissions, triage, reviews and editorial selection. It cannot identify
selection probabilities without submission counts and appropriate strata.

## Actual journal-specific differences

Select a scientifically reasonable rival journal or journal group. Standardize
field, year window, document type and topic classification. A candidate
"difference" is journal-specific only when it is robust to plausible
composition/special-issue controls and is not a field-wide expectation.
Do not infer editor intention from the comparison.

Audit the following independently:
- recurring scientific questions and target systems;
- contribution structure: discovery, mechanism, methodology, prediction,
  demonstration, deployment or cross-scale synthesis;
- claimed novelty relative to nearest prior art;
- direct vs proxy measurements, perturbational evidence, rival hypotheses,
  negative controls, independent validation and external generality;
- article architecture and density of tested claims vs supplementary assays;
- change across matched years and change versus the competitor.

## Evidence-standard shifts

Use a transparent full-text codebook. For each sampled paper:

| Variable | Operational question |
|---|---|
| central claim | Exact primary claim and boundary |
| phenomenon | Descriptive result or measured response? |
| mechanism | Proposed, supported by association, or discriminated through interventions? |
| controls | Which named alternative does each control address? |
| comparators | Does study test the strongest credible prior approach? |
| validation | Internal technical, independent site, temporal or field/scale? |
| replicates | Independent experimental unit and statistical design clear? |
| method strength | Direct measurement, proxy with validation, unsupported proxy? |
| novelty basis | Actual advance versus closest prior art independently checked? |
| limitations | Recorded contradictions, non-generalizability or failure modes? |
| access | full text, abstract-only, or metadata-only |
| reviewer confidence | supported within inspected material, tentative, or not assessable |

Avoid a simplistic "number of omics" or "number of figures" standard.
Multi-omics alone does not demonstrate causal mechanism. Correlative networks,
SEM, feature importance and pathway abundances remain their own evidence class
unless adequately identified.

For a credible change-in-standard claim, use comparable representative or
stratified full-text corpora from early/late periods, multiple example papers
and at least one held-out challenge. Expose coder disagreements and
missing-paywall bias. Calling a feature "mandatory" requires explicit official
guidance or carefully bounded evidence; otherwise say "frequently observed
in the analyzed corpus."

## Rival-journal and held-out challenge

- Test an alleged journal-specific property against comparator articles.
- Select held-out published examples not used to infer the original rule.
- Find counterexamples inside the focal journal and inspect why they differ.
- A "predicts journal of publication" result is not validation of submission
  acceptance probability, because journal assignment is not randomized and
  unpublished submissions are unobserved.
- Editorial rejection heuristics from blogs or author forums must be labeled
  anecdotal, never converted to official policy.

## Scientific frontier handoff

Do not turn "few papers in journal" into "unresolved scientific problem."
Use nature-research-partner to search outside the target journal for strongest
prior art, serious alternative mechanisms, evidence-standard precedent,
counterevidence, Recurring∩Unresolved∩Important∩Tractable, death conditions
and the cheapest P0 experiment.

Return a bounded "what this journal demonstrably published and how those
papers argue" rather than pretending to have decoded private editor intent.
