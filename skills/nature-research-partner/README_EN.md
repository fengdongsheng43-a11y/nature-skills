# `nature-research-partner` Skill

[中文说明](README.md)

`nature-research-partner` is a Scientific Discovery Partner that turns partial inspiration, anomalous observations, and immature research directions into falsifiable, traceable, experimentally testable, and continuously updateable paper-level scientific questions.

## What To Use It For

- Discuss a sudden research idea iteratively instead of immediately completing a convenient story around it.
- Define system boundaries, variables, constraints, objectives, baselines, and evaluation criteria using mathematics or explicit operational definitions.
- Reconstruct the real scientific contradiction, dominant variables, technical failure modes, and key unknowns behind an applied problem.
- Once the idea is clear enough to become searchable, pass a literature-evidence gate before building the five-role coordinate system: mechanism anchor, SOTA, method evidence, counter-evidence, and publication anchor.
- Construct rival hypotheses, discriminating predictions, death conditions, and reviewer-style falsification tests.
- Unify central claims, mechanisms, experiments, measurements, statistical criteria, and conclusions in one evidence architecture.
- Update hypothesis states, evidence ledgers, research decisions, and next experiments when new data or literature arrive.
- Decide whether a direction should continue, narrow, remain unresolved, or stop consuming resources.

## Typical Requests

- “I just had a research idea. Help me clarify the problem before proposing a full plan.”
- “Formalize this idea rigorously and identify the real scientific contradiction.”
- “Build the five-anchor literature coordinate system for this project.”
- “Take the strongest opposing view and try to falsify my claimed novelty.”
- “Which experiments best distinguish these mechanisms instead of merely adding characterization?”
- “Here are the latest data. Update our hypotheses and next-step plan.”

## What You Need To Provide

- Any one of the following is enough to begin: the source of the idea, an anomalous observation, preliminary data, a paper, the research system, or an existing project concept.
- Provide known experimental conditions, equipment, time, budget, or non-negotiable practical constraints when available.
- The initial goal may be vague; the skill first separates known facts, inferences, and unknowns, then progressively tightens the question.

## Outputs

- Research Contract: system, variables, constraints, objectives, baselines, and evidence standards.
- Scientific contradiction: the core tension and answerable research question.
- Literature Evidence Brief: 3–5 high-information anchors, strongest baseline, supported/contradicted/unresolved judgments, and how the evidence changes the initial idea.
- Literature Coordinate: five anchor roles and their evidence boundaries.
- Hypothesis Ledger: candidate mechanisms, rivals, predictions, falsification criteria, and status.
- Evidence Ledger: mapping between claims and direct, adjacent, or inferential evidence.
- Decision Log: why to continue, narrow, pivot, or stop, plus what future result would reverse the decision.
- Master Framework: an evolving paper-level “challenge → mechanism → prediction → experiment → evidence → claim” architecture.

## Workflow

The skill supports four modes: `explore`, `challenge`, `design`, and `update`. In `explore`, a small number of high-value questions first turn the inspiration into a searchable scientific problem. The workflow then passes the literature-evidence gate, returns a Literature Evidence Brief, and discusses which initial assumptions are supported, weakened, or rewritten before moving to rival hypotheses, falsification, and evidence-designed experiments. New evidence then updates the full research state.

## Boundaries

- It does not assume a preferred explanation is correct simply because the user likes it.
- It does not force equations or invented precision onto genuinely unknown variables.
- When retrieval is available, it does not judge SOTA, novelty, mechanism precedent, or method validity from model memory alone.
- It does not treat “no paper found” as proof of “first” or “novel”.
- It does not upgrade correlation, performance gain, or a single characterization result into mechanistic proof.
- It does not use writing or figure packaging as a substitute for unresolved scientific reasoning.

## Related Skills

- `nature-academic-search`: literature discovery, search-boundary documentation, and metadata verification.
- `nature-reader` / `nature-paper-card`: full-text anchor-paper reading and claim–evidence decomposition.
- `nature-statistics`: experimental units, statistical inference, and reporting review.
- `nature-experiment-log`: raw experimental records and research provenance.
- `nature-reviewer`: independent manuscript-level stress testing after the evidence chain exists.
- `nature-writing` / `nature-figure`: manuscript narrative and main-figure production after scientific decisions stabilize.
