# nature-provincial-fund-proposal

Draft v0.1.0. Scientific decision and official policy adaptation for Chinese provincial, autonomous-region and municipality natural science funds, including Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region's student-oriented and general schemes.

Uses [nature-research-partner](../nature-research-partner/SKILL.md) as the non-negotiable evidence and falsification layer and [nature-proposal-writer](../nature-proposal-writer/SKILL.md) for applicant-controlled argument and revision. Region-specific eligibility, local nomination, feasibility, budget, scope and submission rules are read from the **exact call-year guide**; there is no universal provincial template.

**NSFC's Regional Science Fund is national**, so use [nature-nsfc-proposal](../nature-nsfc-proposal/SKILL.md) for that scheme.

Entry: [SKILL.md](SKILL.md), [annual policy intake](references/annual-guide-intake.md), [review gates](references/reviewer-gates.md), and [architecture rationale](../../docs/funding-workflow-architecture.md).
